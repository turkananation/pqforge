## Do you need a commercial license?

You do **not** need a commercial license if you comply with the AGPL-3.0-only.
In short, the AGPL requires that when you distribute software built on
`pqforge` — or let users interact with it **over a network** (SaaS, APIs,
hosted apps) — you must make that software's complete corresponding source
code available under the AGPL as well.

You **do** need a commercial license if any of the following applies:

- You embed `pqforge` in a **closed-source or proprietary** application, SDK,
  device, or firmware that you distribute.
- You use it in a product or service accessed by users over a network and you
  are **unwilling or unable to release that product's source code** under
  AGPL-3.0-only.
- Your organization's policy prohibits AGPL-licensed dependencies.
- You want warranties, indemnification, or support commitments that the AGPL
  (which provides the software "AS IS") does not offer.

If none of these apply — for example, your application is itself open source
under AGPL-3.0-compatible terms — the AGPL grant is free of charge and no
commercial license is needed.
